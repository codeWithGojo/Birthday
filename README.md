Victory Amarachi’s birthday universe, for her twentieth on 13 October 2026.

A starlit sky, photographic planets and a birthday letter at the centre. Caring, personal wording with a gentle romantic hint. Photos, music, twenty notes, English/Russian, wish, quiz, newspaper, credits and final banter remain.

Mobile interaction update:
- Planets float independently; orbital paths move slowly. Pause motion, or use the device’s reduced-motion preference.
- Move planets by dragging one onto another, or by selecting two with touch or keyboard. Positions are saved, and can be reset.
- Surprise me chooses an unexplored planet first.
- Write a shooting-star wish. It stays on the recipient’s device; it is not sent to a server. A meteor carries her nickname across the sky.
- Exploration progress and the last stop survive a refresh.
- Compact phone header, larger touch controls and a single-column photo/video layout. The opening film plays once per browser visit.
