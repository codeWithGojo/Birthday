Happy birthday, Amarachi.

I don't say this enough, but the moments we've shared all this time have meant more to me than you probably know.

I know things haven't always been easy between us, and I don't take it for granted that you've let me back into your life the way you have. Every conversation, every random voice note, every simple "goodnight" — I hold onto those more than you'd guess.

I just want today to be everything good for you. You deserve the peace, the joy, the people who show up for you the way you deserve. I hope this year brings you all the things you've been quietly hoping for.

I love you.

Happy birthday. I mean every word of this.
